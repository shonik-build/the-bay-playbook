WHAT OLDER GUIDES GET WRONG
Amex has required an SSN or ITIN from every US applicant since August 2023. Nova Credit gets your Indian history considered; it no longer gets you around the SSN. So Zolve is the genuine no-SSN move and Amex comes second, after the number arrives. Whether the Nova flow still covers India is harder to confirm than it should be, so check at application time. If you already hold an Indian Amex, ask about Global Transfer instead.

Day-to-day money

Don't convert your Bangalore budget at the exchange rate and call it planning. Rent, healthcare and childcare break naively-converted budgets. Plan for fixed costs eating 50% of income in year one, and re-baseline at month 3 once the one-time setup spend washes out.

Size your emergency fund for visa risk, not just job-loss risk. An RFE or denial can compress your timeline to leave the country, so cover a flight home plus 60 days of US obligations, meaning lease break, final payroll and storage, on top of ordinary living costs.

SENDING MONEY HOME
The margin is in the exchange rate, so compare the rupees landed and never the advertised fee. Wise usually wins that comparison and "zero fee" promos usually don't. The new 1% US remittance tax, from January 2026, only hits cash-funded transfers; anything funded from your bank account is exempt.
APPS, BRIEFLY
YNAB at $109 a year if you want zero-based budgeting while you relearn your cost structure, Quicken Simplifi at about $4 a month if you want lighter-weight. Credit score tracking is free in the Experian app and inside most card apps, so don't pay for monitoring.
