Business banking & moving company money

Which platform to bank on, how to structure accounts once payroll is real, what idle cash should earn, and how money actually moves between your Indian and US entities.

WHAT A FRESH DELAWARE C-CORP CAN OPEN, TODAY, FROM INDIA
{{ w.tag }}
{{ w.what }}
Formation documents, the EIN letter and a passport are enough. The application runs online in about ten minutes, and the account is almost always Mercury. Open it before you fly.
Your personal account and credit file are a separate problem with a separate sequence. That is the Money, credit & ITIN guide. Keep the two strictly separate from day one: commingling is the cheapest mistake to avoid and the most expensive one to unwind.

The four platforms

None of these are banks. All four are fintechs holding your deposits at partner banks, which matters twice: {{ g.fdic.tag }}{{ g.fdic.def }} runs through sweep networks rather than a single institution, and an account freeze gets resolved by the platform's support team rather than a branch manager.

Structure the accounts before you need to

A single checking account is fine for the first six months. The trigger to split is payroll becoming a real line item, not a date.

Moving money: what it actually costs

Mercury and Rho charge nothing for ACH or wires, including international USD wires. This is what a traditional bank charges, and what you pay whenever a counterparty insists on a specific rail.
