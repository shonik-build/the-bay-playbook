What a B-1 lets you do (and what it doesn't)

B-1 is the US business-visitor visa. Most Indian founders get a combined B-1/B-2 stamp.

YOU CAN
Meet investors and pitch them, negotiate contracts, attend conferences and demo days, consult with business associates, scout offices, open conversations with lawyers and accountants. The State Department's own fact sheet lists soliciting investment and negotiating contracts as allowable.
YOU CANNOT
Take US-source salary, do billable work for a US entity, freelance, or "run" a US operation day-to-day. Building your own product on your laptop in a cafe sits in a grey zone; getting paid by anyone in the US does not — it's a clear no. Don't tell the CBP officer you're "moving to SF." You're here for meetings and conferences.
Border officers have tightened B-1 scrutiny since 2025. Carry a return ticket, a printed meeting schedule and proof of ties to India.
