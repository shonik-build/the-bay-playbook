THREE HARD TRUTHS AS OF 2026
1. The H-4 EAD only unlocks after an approved I-140 — typically years away for new arrivals. A newly-landed H-4 spouse cannot work day 1.

2. Auto-extensions are gone. An October 2025 interim rule eliminated automatic extensions for EAD renewals filed on or after 30 Oct 2025. Work authorization ends when the card expires, even if renewal is pending. Renew the full 180 days early; processing runs 5–9 months.

3. O-3 means zero work, full stop — including unpaid work for your own startup. Unauthorized employment wrecks both spouses' immigration records. The workarounds are the spouse getting their own work visa (H-1B, O-1) or switching to F-1 for study.
For a two-career couple, this is often the strongest argument for the spouse pursuing an independent visa. Model both careers before choosing your own category.

Schools: your address is the admission letter

US public schools are free, and enrollment is by home address — each house maps to a specific school. There is no entrance exam and no fee. The "admission process" is the housing market. Families effectively buy or rent their way into a school.

Enrollment mechanics: proof of address (lease), the child's immunization records (US schools require specific vaccines — a US pediatrician can map and top up), and birth certificate. Sequencing: who lands first

FOUNDER SOLO FIRST · 4–8 WEEKS AHEAD
You absorb the chaos — housing, SSN, bank, car — then the family lands into a set-up home. Best when you have a school-age child (land before the school year) or an infant. Cost: separation, and you're doing the school/daycare hunt remotely.
LAND TOGETHER
Better for morale, harder logistics. Book a one-month furnished rental so you're not apartment-hunting with jet-lagged kids.
Avoid landing mid-school-year if you can. August arrival aligns with the US school calendar; December mid-year drops mean the school hunt starts under time pressure.

The #1 underrated risk: spouse isolation

Well-documented in H-4 communities: the founder gets an instant network — investors, cofounders, events. The spouse — often a professional who paused a career — gets an empty apartment in a suburb where they know no one and legally cannot work. Depression and resentment build quietly over months. Treat this as a project with an owner (you) from week one.
