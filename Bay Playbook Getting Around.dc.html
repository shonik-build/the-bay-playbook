Getting around SF (and the Bay)

Living in the city and using transit properly costs about $160 a month. Defaulting to Uber costs closer to $800. That gap is the entire argument for this page.

NOBODY TELLS YOU
San Francisco is not the Bay Area. The city is seven miles by seven at the tip of a peninsula. Palo Alto is 35 miles south, Berkeley is across a bridge, and both are an hour each way. People book a coffee in Mountain View like it's across town and lose the afternoon.

Hills, fog and bikes

Check the elevation before committing to a classic bike. Fillmore north of Broadway is the hill that humbles people; pay the extra $0.30 a minute for an e-bike and stop thinking about it. Cyclists route around the topography rather than through it, which is why the Wiggle exists — a marked zigzag of flat side streets from near Market up to the Panhandle.

The fog is a planning input. Summer evenings in the city sit in the low-to-mid teens Celsius while Palo Alto is at 25. Carry a layer in July. The Sunset and Richmond are colder and greyer than the Mission on the same afternoon, and riding west means a headwind the whole way.

After midnight

BART stops at midnight, every night, Fridays included. Muni's Owl buses run half-hourly until 5am and are the best overnight transit in the region — but a 30-minute wait at 2am in the Tenderloin is a different product from what the map suggests.

Take a Waymo or an Uber. Waymo runs 10–30% above UberX before you account for not tipping, and a lot of people prefer it at night — nobody else in the car and the ride is recorded.

Should you buy a car?

IN THE CITY
No
Garage parking is $350–500 a month in SoMa and downtown, $150–250 in the avenues. Street parking means a residential permit and a sweeping schedule you'll misread twice. Rent from Zipcar, Getaround or Turo for weekends — check each one's policy on foreign licences first.
FREMONT, SUNNYVALE, SANTA CLARA
Yes
South Bay was built around driving, and transit roughly doubles every journey. On break-ins: reported car burglaries are down to roughly a quarter of their 2017 peak and still falling. The rule still holds because what's left is opportunistic — leave nothing visible in a parked car. Not a bag, not a jacket, not a phone charger.

If you're driving: the licence

California gives new residents ten days from establishing residency to hold a California licence, and twenty to register a vehicle. Residency starts when you sign the lease or take the job, so the clock is usually already running.

California has no licence reciprocity with any country. You cannot exchange an Indian licence — you take the written test and the road test as a brand-new driver. Budget for a few lessons if you learned on the left: four-way stops barely exist outside North America, right-on-red is legal here, and examiners fail people for rolling stops constantly.
