One timing note. SF rents peak in July and bottom out in December, a swing of around 4%. You usually can't choose your landing month, but if a lease start can drift a few weeks toward late autumn, let it.

The statewide cap resets from 6.3% to 8.8% on 1 August. Your year-two increase is being decided right now, by which building you pick, not by anything you'll negotiate later. I'd take the older building at slightly higher starting rent almost every time: your rent barely moves in years two through five while market rent climbs 20% or more.

Check any SF building's construction year before you tour, and confirm a specific unit's status with the SF Rent Board on (415) 252-4602. A {{ g.co.tag }}{{ g.co.def }} before 13 June 1979, in a multi-unit building, is what puts an SF unit under city rent control.

Since July 2024, {{ g.ab12.tag }}{{ g.ab12.def }} caps security deposits at one month's rent for most landlords. Bangalore trained you to budget ten months. California went the other way, and "first, last and deposit" is dead too, because last month's rent counts toward the same cap. Individuals who own at most two rental properties totalling four units may still ask for two months. When you leave, the deposit must come back within 21 days, itemised. Note the quiet good news threaded through all of this: every protection here attaches to the tenancy, not the tenant. Your visa status is irrelevant to the deposit cap, the rent caps and the eviction rules.

Before any of them, build a rental resume. One PDF with your visa status, funding announcement or bank balance, LinkedIn, a reference letter from your Indian landlord, and what you're offering as security. Send it with every enquiry, because you are marketing yourself past a missing number. The documents folder in the remote setup guide has you collecting these ingredients before you fly.

Six or more is the whole rule
The deposit cap has exactly one pressure valve written into it: a landlord may accept an advance of six months' rent or more, if the lease runs six months or longer. Below six there is no middle option, and two or three months upfront is just an over-cap deposit the landlord shouldn't take.
Who runs the credit check tells you everything
A landlord runs your credit themselves for that $66 fee. Anyone who instead sends you a link to buy your own credit report is running a scam, not a screening. And building the actual US score starts the week you land, before the SSN arrives.

Scams, and where they start

The FTC counted $65 million in reported rental-scam losses in the year to mid-2025. About half of those started with a fake Facebook ad and another 16% on Craigslist, which is awkward, because Facebook groups and Craigslist are also where the real sublet and small-landlord inventory lives. So don't avoid the channels. Refuse to pay on them.

Scammers now fake legitimacy with self-guided lockbox tours of homes they don't control, so a tour alone proves nothing. Check the owner's name against the county assessor's record or the management company's own site, and reverse-image-search the photos. Rent meaningfully below the chart above is bait, not luck.

AT NIGHT
Be careful around the Tenderloin, Civic Center, and SoMa near 6th Street, with Market around 6th the roughest stretch. It's mostly open drug use and street disorder rather than violence aimed at you, but walk a block over. Street View every exact address before viewing, then walk the block at 9pm before signing. No listing site can do that for you, and neither can this page.
