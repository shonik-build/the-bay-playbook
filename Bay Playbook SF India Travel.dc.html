FARES · MID-2026
Economy round trip runs $1,000–1,400 SFO↔BLR/MAA on Gulf/Singapore carriers in normal months. SFO↔DEL sometimes dips to $650–900. December (Dec 10 – Jan 5) spikes to $1,800–2,500+ — the NRI rush.
WHEN TO BOOK
2–3 months out for normal trips. Book December travel by August or September. Set Google Flights alerts on your two home-city routes permanently — the alerts catch the ~30% dips that don't publish anywhere else.
