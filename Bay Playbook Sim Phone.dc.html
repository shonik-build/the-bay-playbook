Keep your Indian number for OTPs

Every Indian bank, mutual fund, Aadhaar-linked service and family WhatsApp group is tied to your Indian number. Losing it locks you out of your own money from a US IP.

AIRTEL / JIO INTERNATIONAL ROAMING
Enable in the app before you fly. Cheapest tier (~₹500/month) covers SMS — enough for OTPs. Don't upgrade to data plans; they're 20× the cost of a local eSIM.
WHATSAPP KEEPS THE NUMBER
Your WhatsApp identity stays on your Indian number even after you switch to a US SIM. No migration needed — the app just verifies via SMS on first login, which is why roaming matters.
