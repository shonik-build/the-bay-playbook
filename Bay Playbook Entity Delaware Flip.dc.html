The Indian-side rules: ODI 2022 and round-tripping

The {{ g.odi.tag }}{{ g.odi.def }} (issued August 2022) govern Indians acquiring foreign shares. Two things matter for a flip.

ROUND-TRIPPING, WITH CONDITIONS
The old blanket ban on structures where a foreign entity owns an Indian one is gone. Now permitted for bona fide business — but only up to two layers of subsidiaries, and only where the US parent has genuine substance: real US customers, US operations, a documented commercial rationale. A shell Delaware parent existing purely to hold Indian shares invites RBI trouble.
THE SWAP USES YOUR LRS HEADROOM
The {{ g.lrs.tag }}{{ g.lrs.def }} caps each resident Indian at $250,000 per financial year for overseas investment. Acquiring US parent shares is an ODI transaction under these rules, valued by a SEBI-registered merchant banker. Budget the valuation report at ₹1–2 lakh and FEMA/RBI filings (Form FC, annual APR) at ₹1–3 lakh in professional fees.
FEMA is India's Foreign Exchange Management Act — the law behind all of this. ODI is Overseas Direct Investment — an Indian resident buying into a foreign company.

The warning label: reverse flips are brutal

$600M+ collectively paid to un-flip.
In 2024–25, Meesho, Groww and Razorpay collectively paid $600M+ in US taxes to un-flip and move HQ back to India for domestic IPOs. Meesho paid ~$288M; Groww ~$160M. If your realistic exit is an India listing, think hard before flipping at all. The flip is close to a one-way door once value builds up in the Delaware parent.
The decision is not "do I flip?" — it is "is a US listing my realistic exit?" If the answer is no or unclear, delay the flip until the fundraising forces it.

ESOPs during the flip — the section nobody warns you about

Options are not shares. This is the sentence that catches most founders out. When you do a share swap, shareholders swap; optionholders do not, because they hold a contract, not a security. Your ESOP does not travel with the flip automatically. It has to be moved deliberately, by paperwork, and every unvested and vested option in the pool needs to be accounted for.

EARLY-EXERCISED SHARES ARE A SEPARATE PROBLEM
Employees who exercised early are shareholders. They participate in the swap, which means each becomes an ODI filer with their own LRS headroom, their own valuation compliance and their own Form FC. Twelve employees who exercised is twelve extra sets of FEMA filings. Keep the pre-flip cap table as thin as you can.

PRESERVE THE VESTING COMMENCEMENT DATE IN WRITING
Whichever route you take. If the regrant paperwork resets the clock, an employee three years into a four-year vest starts again — and you will find out about it in the worst possible way. The vesting commencement date is the joining date, and it is the number that actually matters in a regrant.
