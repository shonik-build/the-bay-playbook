READ THIS BEFORE YOU APPLY ANYWHERE
Nobody sponsors your visa, and acceptance is not a criterion
The standard path on an Indian passport is a B-1 visitor visa for the batch, because ESTA isn't available to Indian passports, then O-1A for the long term. Getting into one of these strengthens an O-1 petition but doesn't satisfy any criterion on its own. Start your evidence file, meaning press, funding, judging and published work, six to twelve months before you need to work legally, and get a real immigration lawyer rather than a forum thread.
TWO PROGRAMS GO FURTHER
Activate and Antler require existing US work authorisation just to participate, which rules out most new arrivals. They're flagged in the list below and excluded automatically if you filter for working from India.
THE OTHER PRECONDITION
Most invest only in Delaware C-corps, or a short list of accepted foreign parents like Cayman and Singapore. An Indian private limited company means a flip, and a flip is much harder to undo than to do.
The flip triggers Indian tax on the share swap plus FEMA filings. Get the cross-border consult before an acceptance forces the timeline, not after.

WHAT THE CHART CAN'T SHOW
Signal. YC is mid-table on this measure and still the strongest signal in the market, which is most of what you're buying. Follow-on money doesn't show either: South Park Commons guarantees $600K into your next round and a16z Speedrun puts in another $500K within 18 months, so both are better than they look here. Use the chart to catch a genuinely bad trade, not to rank the good ones.
