Two things that stall week one

No US number = no bank, no Uber, no anything
Activate the eSIM before landing so you have connectivity from the airport curb. Every subsequent step (bank OTP, Uber login, Zelle) fails without it.
A US address unlocks half the paperwork
The hotel booking or short-term lease counts. Save the address on your phone — you'll type it 30+ times in week one for bank forms, delivery addresses, SSN applications and every "what's your address?" moment.
